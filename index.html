once

A private ledger for a home poker game.

once keeps the books for your table: who bought in, who added on, who cashed out, and who owes whom when the night ends.

what it tracks

  • Buy-ins, add-ons and cash-outs, logged as they happen
  • Settlements worked out at the end, so nobody does mental arithmetic at 2am
  • A running history and leaderboard across every session
  • A feed and group chat for the table talk between games

it does not handle money

once is a record, not a payment app. It never processes, holds or moves funds. It works out what players already agreed between themselves, and players settle up directly in whichever payment app they use.

who it is for

Anyone who hosts or plays in a regular home game. Sign in, start a session, share the code with your table, and once keeps the ledger. Your games and history are visible only to the players in them.

For adults aged 18 and over.
